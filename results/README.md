# results

Released case-level split identifiers. Every other result is regenerated by the scripts (see the main README); the article reports its results in the main text and Online Resource 1.

* `split_ids/split_ids_pooled_premise.csv.gz`: the train/validation/test split of the pooled 2016-2024 machine-learning experiment (Table 7 and Online Resource 1, Tables S9a to S13b, both BAC definitions), for each of the five seeds. 453,096 cases.
* `split_ids/split_ids_identification_cohort.csv.gz`: the same split rule applied to the identification cohort (cohort-harmonization check). 468,574 cases.

Columns: `CASE_ID` (CRSS data year and CASENUM, `<year>_<CASENUM>`) and `seed_42`, `seed_1337`, `seed_2026`, `seed_31415`, `seed_271828`, each `train`, `val` or `test`. Cases, not drivers, are split (70/10/20, stratified on whether the case had a fatally injured driver), so all drivers of a crash fall in the same part. `3_ml_premise/ml_premise_temporal_annual.py` regenerates these splits and also writes the splits of the temporal and annual experiments.
